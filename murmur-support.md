# Wristape · 腕声 — Support

Wristape records your day on Apple Watch; your iPhone transcribes it on device, tells voices apart, and reviews each day for you. This page is the support page for the app.

## Help
- **Recording**: open Wristape on your watch and tap to start. Tap again to mark a moment; hold for a second to stop. You can also start from the complication, the Action button or Siri. Swipe up for today's totals and to pause for 15 minutes.
- **Getting recordings to iPhone**: recordings move to your iPhone every few minutes while it's nearby. If it's out of reach they wait on the watch; the watch stops recording before it runs out of space.
- **Transcripts and reviews**: transcription happens on your iPhone, often while it's idle or charging. Reviews use Apple Intelligence, or a cloud model you set up in Settings with your own key.
- **Backup**: your recordings aren't in the iPhone's own backup. Use Settings > Backup to save a backup file somewhere you trust.
- **Privacy**: there is no account and no server. See `murmur-privacy.md` in this repository.
- **Requirements**: iPhone with iOS 26 or later, paired with an Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later.

# 腕声 Wristape — 技术支持

腕声在 Apple Watch 上记录你的一天，iPhone 在本机转写、分辨不同的声音，并为你回顾每一天。

## 常见问题
- **录音**：在手表上打开腕声，轻点开始。再轻点标记一个时刻，长按一秒停止。也可以从表盘复杂功能、操作按钮或 Siri 开始。向上轻扫查看今天的统计，或暂停 15 分钟。
- **录音传到 iPhone**：iPhone 在附近时，录音每隔几分钟传过去一次。不在附近时先留在手表上；手表空间快满时会停止录音。
- **转写和回顾**：转写在 iPhone 上完成，常在空闲或充电时进行。回顾使用 Apple 智能，或你在"设置"里用自己的 Key 配置的云端模型。
- **备份**：录音不在 iPhone 自带的备份里。请在"设置 > 备份"里把备份文件存到你信任的地方。
- **隐私**：没有账号，也没有服务器。见本仓库的 `murmur-privacy.md`。
- **要求**：iOS 26 及以上的 iPhone，配对 watchOS 26 及以上的 Apple Watch。
