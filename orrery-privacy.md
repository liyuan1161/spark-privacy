# Orrery Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Developer: Ledi (乐迪)

Orrery is a watch face app for Apple Watch. **It does not collect any data.** It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and makes no network requests.

## What stays on your watch
Your chosen face and colour scheme are saved in the app's own storage (UserDefaults) on your Apple Watch. They never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Complications
The complications (Mini Orrery, Moon Phase, Day Progress) are computed on the watch from the current date and time only. The moon phase is calculated, not downloaded. No location is used.

## Children
Orrery does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, a new version will be published at this address with an updated date.

# 星盘 Orrery 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 19 日

开发者：乐迪

星盘是一款 Apple Watch 表盘 App。**它不收集任何数据。**没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析、没有跟踪，也不进行任何网络请求。

## 保存在手表上的内容
你选择的表盘和配色保存在 App 自己的存储（UserDefaults）中，只留在你的 Apple Watch 上，不会上传。删除 App 即会一并删除。

## 复杂功能
迷你星盘、月相、今日进度这几个复杂功能都只根据当前日期和时间在手表上计算得出。月相是计算出来的，不需要下载数据，也不使用位置信息。

## 儿童
本 App 不会有意向任何人（包括儿童）收集个人信息。

## 政策变更
如本政策有变更，会在此地址发布新版本并更新日期。
