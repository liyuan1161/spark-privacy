# Emberfall · 残焰 — Support

Emberfall is an offline pixel-art action roguelike for iPhone. This page is the support page for the app.

## Help
- **Controls**: slide the thumb pad (bottom left) to move; use the buttons (bottom right) to attack, jump, roll and drink a flask.
- **Progress**: cells collected in a run are kept when you die and can be spent on permanent upgrades from the menu. Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes it.
- **Languages**: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어 (follows your iPhone language).
- **Requirements**: iPhone with iOS 16 or later. No internet connection needed.

# 残焰 Emberfall — 技术支持

《残焰》是一款面向 iPhone 的离线像素风动作 Roguelike。

## 常见问题
- **操作**：左下滑动方向条移动；右下按钮负责攻击、跳跃、翻滚、使用药瓶。
- **进度**：每局收集的细胞在死亡后保留，可在菜单中购买永久强化。进度只保存在设备上，删除 App 会同时删除。
- **语言**：English、简体中文、繁體中文、日本語、한국어，跟随 iPhone 系统语言。
- **要求**：iOS 16 及以上的 iPhone，无需联网。
