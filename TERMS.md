## 3. Health and Safety

Flashing lights and photosensitive epilepsy: Rhythm Flash, the SOS signal in Spark Light, the Neon Sign blink effect, and the Metronome's flash all contain rapidly flashing light. A very small number of people may have seizures when exposed to flashing lights. If you or anyone in your family has a history of epilepsy or light sensitivity, talk to a doctor before use. Stop immediately and seek medical help if you feel dizzy or notice altered vision, eye or muscle twitching, or confusion. Don't shine the flashlight or a bright screen directly into anyone's eyes, including your own.

Movement and motion: Some games ask you to tilt or shake your phone, shout, or control the game with your eyes or hand gestures in the air. Hold your phone firmly, play somewhere safe and open, and watch out for people, pets, and objects around you. Don't play while walking, cycling, driving, or doing anything else that needs your attention. When shouting, be considerate of others and look after your voice. When using Stair Climber, focus on walking, watch your step, and don't stare at the screen while you climb.

Use in moderation: Take regular breaks to rest your eyes. Keeping the flashlight on or the screen at full brightness for a long time can make your device warm; pause if it feels hot. Parents and guardians should help minors keep a healthy amount of screen time.
