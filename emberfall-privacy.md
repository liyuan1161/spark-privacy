# Emberfall Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Emberfall is an offline game. It has no account system, no ads and no analytics, and it does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

## What stays on your device
Your progress (cells, permanent upgrades, deepest floor reached) is stored locally in the app's own storage (UserDefaults). It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## What the app does not do
- It does not collect personal information, identifiers, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
- It does not use third-party SDKs, advertising or tracking.
- It does not connect to the internet.

## Haptics and sound
The app uses the device's haptic engine and plays sound effects and music generated on the device. No audio is recorded.

## Children
The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

## Contact
Please open an issue at https://github.com/liyuan1161/spark-privacy/issues

# 《残焰》隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日

《残焰》是一款离线游戏。它没有账号系统、没有广告、没有统计分析，也不会收集、传输或分享任何个人数据。

## 保存在你设备上的内容
你的游戏进度（细胞、永久强化、最深到达层数）仅保存在 App 自己的本地存储（UserDefaults）中，不会发送到任何地方。删除 App 即同时删除这些数据。

## App 不会做的事
- 不收集个人信息、标识符、位置、通讯录、照片，也不使用相机或麦克风。
- 不使用第三方 SDK、广告或跟踪。
- 不连接互联网。

## 触感与声音
App 会使用设备的触感引擎，并播放在设备上实时合成的音效和音乐，不会录音。

## 儿童
本 App 不向任何人（包括儿童）收集任何信息。

## 政策变更
如本政策有变更，会在此地址发布新版本并更新日期。

## 联系方式
请在 GitHub 提交 issue：https://github.com/liyuan1161/spark-privacy/issues
