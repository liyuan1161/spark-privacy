# Emberfall Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Emberfall is a single-player game. It has no account system and no ads. It uses Google's Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics so we can improve the game. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## What stays on your device
Your progress (cells, permanent upgrades, deepest floor reached) is stored locally in the app's own storage (UserDefaults) and is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Anonymous analytics
The app uses Firebase Analytics (Google LLC). It receives:
- **Product interaction**: gameplay events such as starting a run, reaching a floor, defeating a guardian, ending a run (floor, kills, cells, duration) and buying an upgrade.
- **Device identifier**: a random app-instance ID generated by Firebase, plus basic technical information such as app version, OS version, device model, language and approximate country.

This data is not linked to your identity, is used only for analytics, and is not used for advertising or tracking. The app does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA). Data is processed by Google under [Firebase's privacy terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Because the data is anonymous, we cannot look up or delete data for a specific person; deleting the app stops all further collection.

## Haptics and sound
The app uses the device's haptic engine and plays sound effects and music generated on the device. No audio is recorded.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

## Contact
Please open an issue at https://github.com/liyuan1161/spark-privacy/issues

# 《残焰》隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 18 日

《残焰》是一款单机游戏，没有账号系统，没有广告。我们使用 Google 的 Firebase Analytics 收集匿名使用统计，用于改进游戏。我们不收集你的姓名、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片，也不使用相机或麦克风，不会跨其他公司的 App 或网站跟踪你。

## 保存在你设备上的内容
你的游戏进度（细胞、永久强化、最深到达层数）仅保存在 App 自己的本地存储（UserDefaults）中，不会上传。删除 App 即同时删除这些数据。

## 匿名统计
App 使用 Firebase Analytics（Google LLC），会收到：
- **产品交互**：游戏事件，例如开始一局、到达某层、击败守卫、结束一局（层数、击杀数、细胞数、时长）、购买强化。
- **设备标识符**：由 Firebase 生成的随机应用实例 ID，以及 App 版本、系统版本、设备型号、语言、大致国家等基本技术信息。

这些数据不与你的身份关联，仅用于统计分析，不用于广告或跟踪。App 不读取广告标识符（IDFA）。数据由 Google 按 [Firebase 隐私条款](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)处理。由于数据是匿名的，我们无法按个人查询或删除；删除 App 即停止后续收集。

## 触感与声音
App 会使用设备的触感引擎，并播放在设备上实时合成的音效和音乐，不会录音。

## 儿童
本 App 不会有意向任何人（包括儿童）收集个人信息。

## 政策变更
如本政策有变更，会在此地址发布新版本并更新日期。

## 联系方式
请在 GitHub 提交 issue：https://github.com/liyuan1161/spark-privacy/issues
