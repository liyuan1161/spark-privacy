# Orrery · 星盘 — Support

Orrery is a collection of watch faces for Apple Watch. This page is the support page for the app.

## Help
- **Switch faces**: turn the Digital Crown (or swipe up and down).
- **Change colours**: tap the face. Each face has three colour schemes, and your choice is remembered.
- **Keep Orrery on screen**: on your watch, open Settings › General › Return to Clock, choose Orrery and set it to "After 1 hour". Otherwise watchOS goes back to the system face after a couple of minutes.
- **Always On**: when you lower your wrist, the face switches to a dark, low-power version without seconds.
- **Complications**: touch and hold your watch face, tap Edit, swipe to Complications and pick Mini Orrery, Moon Phase or Day Progress from Orrery.
- **Why can't Orrery replace the system watch face?** watchOS does not allow third-party apps to create system watch faces. Orrery is an app that shows full-screen faces, plus complications for the system faces.
- **Languages**: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어 (follows your watch language).
- **Requirements**: Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. No iPhone app or internet connection needed.

# 星盘 Orrery — 技术支持

星盘是一组 Apple Watch 表盘。

## 常见问题
- **切换表盘**：转动数码表冠，或上下轻扫。
- **更换配色**：轻点表盘。每个表盘有三套配色，选择会被记住。
- **让星盘一直显示**：在手表上打开「设置 › 通用 › 返回时钟」，选择星盘，设为「1 小时后」。否则 watchOS 会在几分钟后回到系统表盘。
- **常亮显示**：放下手腕时，表盘会切换为不显示秒针的暗色省电样式。
- **复杂功能**：长按表盘，轻点「编辑」，滑到「复杂功能」，从星盘中选择迷你星盘、月相或今日进度。
- **为什么不能替换系统表盘？** watchOS 不允许第三方 App 制作系统表盘。星盘是一个全屏显示表盘的 App，并为系统表盘提供复杂功能。
- **语言**：English、简体中文、繁體中文、日本語、한국어，跟随手表系统语言。
- **要求**：watchOS 10 及以上的 Apple Watch，无需 iPhone App，无需联网。
